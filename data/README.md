## Datasets

### ChestX-ray14
- 112,120 chest X-ray images
- 14 disease labels
- Source: NIH Clinical Center
- [Kaggle Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nih-chest-xrays/data)

### Indiana University Chest X-ray Dataset (IU X-Ray)
- Chest X-ray images paired with radiology reports
- Source: Indiana University / NLM OpenI
- [Kaggle Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/raddar/chest-xrays-indiana-university)

### OpenI (OpenAccess)
- Chest X-ray images with clinical radiology reports
- Source: National Library of Medicine
- [NLM OpenI](https://openi.nlm.nih.gov/)

> The complete datasets are not included in this repository because of their large size. The datasets were downloaded from Kaggle for project development and analysis.
